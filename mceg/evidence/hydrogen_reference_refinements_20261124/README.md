# Matched hydrogen reference refinements and boundary qualification

This supplements evidence/qualified-hydrogen-reference-20261124 without
replacing its source, roots or failure records. The figures are byte-identical
to the images inside that archive and are separate assets for PR rendering.
They show a pure-H prescribed-temperature slab, not a planetary fit.

The native OU2 continuum frequency order4-to-8 control actually re-solves
all 267 cells. The fine root residual is 2.29683e-12, and maximum changes
in n2, n3 and electrons are 0.03407%, 0.198665% and 0.001821%, respectively,
below the unchanged 1% criterion. This advances the earlier fixed-state
frequency diagnostic; solar composition and thermal roots remain separate.

A separate unmodified C23.01 forward/reverse illumination-keyword control
has 127 zones and six complete columns. The requested options appear in
the executed input echo, but all five saved physical tables are byte-identical.
Source field references and the unchanged binary/input hashes are retained.
This does not prove that future versions or every illumination option are
inactive. It does show that keyword acceptance alone cannot establish a
matched lower incident boundary in this tested build/control.

The native lower LTE cells emit into the upper atmosphere. Its dark inner
boundary is at the bottom of the whole model. Removing those cells and
starting a separate upper-column reference in the dark changes the problem.
The included plan therefore begins with a matched-state/radiation operator
comparison; no unexecuted upper-column equivalence is claimed.

No original stellar spectral arrays, binaries, objects, caches or compiler
IR are included. Incident spectra in these controls are analytic blackbodies.
All source/configuration identities and original observations of failures
remain in the corresponding archives.
