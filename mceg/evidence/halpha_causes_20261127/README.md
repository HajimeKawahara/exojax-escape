# Candidate causes of the phase-local H-alpha contrast

The strongest new structural clue is the extended absorbing region in free
model 1. Above 1.725 Rp its static radial line-center optical depth is 4.88,
compared with 0.0456 in the physical model. Model 1 keeps appreciable excited
hydrogen outside a bright emitting region, while its source function drops.
This is a descriptive comparison between different atmosphere/observer
families, not an isolated causal experiment or a validated new atmosphere.
The [radial structure figure](radial_structure.png) shows the stored n=2 and
source-function profiles; [PDF](radial_structure.pdf) is available for export.

The [report](../../../doc/halpha_causes.pdf) compares the same complete
0.37615–0.39322 exposures and full native data. It separates fixed-parameter
responses from fits that profile the same two global RV/amplitude parameters.
No new phase cut or production model is adopted.

`summary.json` and the report table are regenerated by `python
mceg/evidence/halpha_causes_20261127/summarize.py`. Family READMEs give their
replay commands and numerical-control scope. Full arrays remain in the
hash-recorded local caches; these receipts do not redistribute the archived
observations. `protocol.json` records the post-hoc target and comparison rules.

Source-only responses use fine rays at all seven phase nodes. Opacity
responses refine the nodes bracketing the target, with their narrower
all-phase convergence limit explicitly retained. Velocity/longitude
refinements are separate from the unresolved eight-cell atmospheric
stratification. The two pixel interleaves are correlated sensitivity checks.

The next controlled comparison should put the free and physical radial
source/opacity profiles through the same observer, separating extended upper
opacity from the location of the source transition. Any physical explanation
must then maintain the required n=2 reservoir and n=3/n=2 ratio through its
population, energy and flow equations. Irradiation, shielding and spatial
density/flow structure are hypotheses, not detected mechanisms. Calibrated
noise and repeated-phase controls remain unresolved.
