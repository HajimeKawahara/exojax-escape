\maketitle

The three retained H-alpha transitions have different rest frequencies.
The current observer uses their $n,l$ energy differences consistently in the
line profiles, thermal widths, absorption and emission. The scalar NIST center
continues to define the output wavelength grid and reported velocity origin.
This changes the observer of a supplied atmosphere; it does not change the
coupled rate, radiation, thermal or wind equations. The
\href{solar_atmosphere.pdf}{solar-atmosphere document} defines that coupled model.

The \href{nlte_spectrum.pdf}{original observer document} and its pinned
verification remain unchanged. Its common-center formula is revision 1;
revision 2 below records the current branch centers. Neither definition alone
establishes physical accuracy, numerical convergence or complete-path derivatives.
The three retained $n,l$ centers do not resolve full fine-$J$ structure.
Pressure/Stark/self broadening remain separate omissions.

\section*{Connection to the code}
The contract \texttt{escape.nlte\_spectrum.yaml} stores this equation and its
symbols; this fragment is generated from \texttt{branch\_profiles}, revision 2.
In \texttt{src/escape/nlte\_forward.py},
\texttt{HydrogenNLTEForwardModel} obtains the three centers from
\texttt{atom.line\_frequency\_hz[2:5]/c}.
Binding \texttt{branch\_weights\_nl\_centers} names
\texttt{halpha\_coefficients}, which uses them in both coefficient prefactors.
Binding \texttt{branch\_voigt\_nl\_centers} names
\texttt{HydrogenNLTEForwardModel.\_line\_profiles}, which uses them in the
detuning and thermal width. Full upper/lower radiative lifetimes are unchanged.
MCEG records these definitions and correspondences without becoming a runtime
dependency or executing the atmosphere calculation.

The separate scalar-center coefficient interface remains available for explicit
comparison controls. Historical common-center support must not be transferred
to the revised observer without source-qualified numerical evidence.
