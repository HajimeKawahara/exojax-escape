\textbf{What is now recorded?}
A quantity has a physical meaning, units and coordinates. A relation adopts
an equation or another mathematical statement. A model chooses relations
and explicitly connects their local symbols to shared quantities. The
canonical record is
\texttt{mceg/contracts/escape.mathematical\_model.yaml}.
Its three models are selected components of the existing implementation,
not a claim that every atmosphere and observation equation has been formalized.

\textbf{Equal units do not mean equal quantities.}
The wind wrapper passes $T/\mu$ to its temperature-profile solver with the
solver's mean molecular weight set to one. The gas temperature $T$ and the
pressure-equivalent temperature $T_{\rm eff}$ both have units K, but are
different quantities. Their conversion is a recorded relation, rather than
an alias inferred from the argument name \texttt{temperature}.
Similarly, $\rho$ is total mass density and $n_{\rm H}$ counts hydrogen nuclei;
their relation includes the mass per hydrogen nucleus of the adopted mixture.
The wind component treats $T$ and $\mu$ as supplied profiles. The complete
solar model determines them using additional composition and thermal equations.

\textbf{The radiation fields are deliberately distinct.}
The returned \texttt{continuum.mean\_intensity} is the native slab diagnostic.
The returned \texttt{mean\_intensity} is the actual continuum field after
selected frequencies have been replaced by a complete shared field. That
replacement is not an additional source added on top of the old intensity.
The continuum pumping approximation uses a forward-diffuse construction
off the replacement mask, and discrete line pumping has a different frequency
grid and finite-cell average. These fields must not all be connected merely
because the notation resembles $J$.

For the selected solar path, the shared field can come from the declared
spherical shell-average calculation. That outer component, line escape
probability, selected line overlap, metals and thermal closure retain their
existing contracts. The new continuum graph has named coefficient and
transport interfaces; it does not expand all their atomic equations.
The current solar path uses local statistical equilibrium without population
advection. A graph cycle does not change that physical approximation.

\textbf{What the GP graph does and does not change.}
The atmosphere is still the saved state used by the previous conditional fit.
RV and contrast are shared planet parameters; GP amplitudes, time lengths
and white scales vary by night/order. The common nightly spectrum is removed
from the mean and covariance in the same fixed contrast measure.
The exposure-centre Mat\'ern kernel includes the actual $10^{-10}$ training
regularizer scaled by amplitude squared. This small numerical covariance
modification is recorded explicitly. The GP and planet priors are distribution
relations. NUTS, MAP initialization and Woodbury factorization are numerical
methods; no new inference is performed by this metadata update.
The native observation operator has an explicit interface connecting the
sampled RV and contrast to the prediction from the saved template; its
internal equations remain in the existing observation contracts.
See \texttt{doc/time\_gp.pdf} for the inference equations and observational limits.

\textbf{How this connects to MCEG verification.}
An Equation Binding points to a recorded source function and its declared
arguments. A Claim states what correspondence is to be tested. Evidence
contains an executed comparison; an Assessment records whether that scoped
claim is supported, contradicted or unknown. The mathematical model hash
includes its quantities, relation definitions and symbol connections.
Connecting photoionization to the old slab diagnostic instead of the selected
field changes the hash even though the units are unchanged.

The authoring checks verify declared identities, reject an incomplete symbol
map and demonstrate that a wrong but unit-compatible connection changes the
model pin. Existing finite wind and dense-GP comparisons are rerun separately.
These checks do not establish the complete code correspondence of all three
graphs. That larger claim remains \textbf{unknown}. Existing solar and GP
Assessments and nominal-use requirements retain their historical records;
this update does not promote physical readiness.

\textbf{Reproduce the records.}
Use MCEG schema 0.5 in a separate authoring environment. The provider package
does not import or depend on MCEG. From the repository root:
\begin{verbatim}
python mceg/checks/verify_mathematical_model.py
mceg model mceg/contracts/escape.mathematical_model.yaml \
  --model continuum_feedback --revision 1 --format mermaid
mceg model mceg/contracts/escape.mathematical_model.yaml \
  --model composition_wind --revision 1 --format tex \
  --output doc/mathematical_wind_generated.tex --check
\end{verbatim}
Omit \texttt{--check} when regenerating the TeX fragment. Equivalent commands
export the other two models; their full quantity tables remain in the YAML
and CLI output instead of being duplicated in this short explanation.
