Changes: native residual ratios are transformed into log flux; a shared
weighted time-constant spectrum is removed separately per night, order and
wavelength. The primary source-map comparison retains phases 0.30 <= phase
< 0.435. Spectral display bins use the same data/model transformation and
mask. Model source maps redistribute a prescribed local emissivity at fixed
opacity. These are diagnostics, not new thermochemical atmosphere solutions.
The eclipse panel additionally reforms references from disjoint or nested
late-exposure sets and propagates conditional shared-reference uncertainty.
Derived plots and transformations are explained in the PR; no endorsement
by the observational creator is implied. These figures are provided with
CC BY 4.0 attribution for their observational content.
